Cybersecurity Analyst | From Building Codes to Threat Detection

I have always been a "high-resolution thinker"—someone who operates at the intersection of creative design and rigorous technical problem-solving. Long before my decade-long career as an architect, my affinity for details manifested in a lifelong habit of tinkering with computers. From establishing a personal data backup culture that has preserved my files for 18 years, to manually hunting down file-exfiltrating malware to protect my university peers, my innate security mindset has always been active.

Today, I bring that combined technical intuition and architectural mindset to cybersecurity. Just as I once navigated structural tolerances and project administration, I now leverage frameworks like NIST and ISO 27001 to architect secure digital environments while focusing on detection, triaging, and investigating security alerts.

Defending an organization isn't just about deploying tools. It’s about understanding the deep, structural details where vulnerabilities hide, and orchestrating people, policy, and technology to build unshakeable defenses.
